👋 Welcome

Imus Heritage Park is a memorial park in Barangay Alapan 2-A, in the city of Imus, Cavite, Philippines. It is built at the place traditionally identified as the battlefield of the Battle of Alapan, fought on May 28, 1898. That battle was the first big military victory of General Emilio Aguinaldo after he came home from exile in Hong Kong, and it is remembered as the day the Philippine flag was first unfurled in triumph. 🚩

Because of this history, Imus is nicknamed the "Flag Capital of the Philippines." Every year, the National Flag Days celebration, which runs from May 28 to June 12, opens at this very park.

The park is easy to spot from far away because of its very tall flagpole, and it also has a flag shrine, a sanctum for old revolutionary flags, an amphitheater, and a giant statue called Inang Laya.

⭐ Why Visit?
🇵🇭 Birthplace of a symbolThis is where the flag we know today was first raised after a battle victory.
🚩 A very tall flagpoleThe pole once stood at Rizal Park in Manila before it was moved here.
🗽 Big public artThe 10-meter Inang Laya statue stands at the center of the amphitheater.
📚 Learn history on siteThe markers, shrine, and flag sanctum explain the story of the revolution.
🎭 Flag Day eventsCeremonies and reenactments are held here during National Flag Days.
